3 Easy Steps to Start

Get set up in under 60 seconds with simple instructions.

01

Grant Notification Access

Enable Android's Notification Listener permission so the app can read incoming notification headers & body text.

02

Set Unrestricted Battery

Select 'Unrestricted' under battery settings to ensure Android keeps the sound receiver active even when the app is closed.

03

Add Keyword & Choose Sound

Tap '+' in DNotify Manager, type your keyword (e.g., "Bank"), pick a tune or custom ringtone, and tap Add!

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DNotify Manager.

How does DNotify Manager catch notifications?
DNotify Manager uses Android's official NotificationListenerService API. It inspects incoming notification titles and content against your defined rules locally on your device.
Does the app send my notification data anywhere?
No! All notification processing occurs 100% locally on your phone. DNotify Manager never uploads, stores, or transmits your messages to any server. Read our Privacy Policy for full commitments.
Why should I set Battery to Unrestricted?
Android aggressive battery optimizations can pause background services. Setting DNotify Manager to 'Unrestricted' ensures your sound alerts play instantly even when your phone is sleeping or the app UI is closed.
Can I stop long playing ringtones easily?
Yes! When a tune plays, DNotify Manager inserts a clear notification card with a prominent "STOP" button into your status shade. Tapping "STOP" instantly silences the audio.
Is keyword matching case-sensitive?
No, matching is case-insensitive. A keyword rule for "bank" will match "Bank", "BANK", or "bank".
